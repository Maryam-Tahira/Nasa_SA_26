🔬 Latest Space Research

Current research areas and recent papers in space science & astronomy

🌟 Hot Research Areas (2025-2026)

🌌 Early Universe with JWST ACTIVE

JWST is discovering galaxies that formed far earlier than expected — challenging models of cosmic evolution. Some galaxies appear "too massive, too soon" after the Big Bang.

🪐 Exoplanet Atmospheres ACTIVE

JWST spectroscopy is detecting water, CO₂, methane, and even possible biosignatures (like DMS on K2-18b) in exoplanet atmospheres — hunting for signs of life.

🌑 Dark Energy Surveys ACTIVE

DESI (Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument) released results suggesting dark energy may be weakening over time — potentially rewriting cosmology.

🌕 Lunar Science & Artemis ACTIVE

Preparing for crewed Moon landings — studying lunar water ice, regolith for construction (ISRU), and radiation shielding for long-duration stays.

🔴 Mars Sample Return PLANNED

Perseverance is caching rock samples. NASA & ESA planning missions to bring them to Earth — could reveal if Mars ever hosted life.

🤖 AI in Astronomy ACTIVE

Machine learning classifies galaxies, detects exoplanets, finds gravitational lenses, and analyzes petabytes of survey data — transforming the field.
