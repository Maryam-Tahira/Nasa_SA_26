🏗️ NASA Rocket History & Current Programs

🚀

Saturn V (1967-1973)

Apollo Moon missions · 13 launches

110.6m tall. Most powerful until SLS. Carried 12 humans to Moon. 7.5M lbs thrust. ~$1.16B per launch (today).
🛸

Space Shuttle (1981-2011)

135 missions · Built the ISS

First reusable craft. 5 orbiters. Built ISS, serviced Hubble. 355 people to space. Columbia & Challenger lost.
🔥

SLS (Active)

Artemis program · Most powerful flying

98m, 8.8M lbs thrust. Artemis I done Nov 2022. Artemis II (crewed flyby) & III (landing) upcoming. RS-25 engines.
🏗️

Artemis IV+ (Building)

Block 2 SLS · Deep space

Will be most powerful ever. EUS in development. Will launch Gateway modules and support Mars missions.
⚡

SpaceX Falcon 9/Heavy

NASA's crew launcher · 300+ launches

70m, reusable. 200+ landings. Carries astronauts via Crew Dragon. Starship selected as Artemis lander.
🌟

Starship (Testing)

121m · Full reusability · Mars capable

Tallest ever. 16.7M lbs thrust (2× Saturn V). Both stages reusable. Artemis HLS lander. Goal: multiplanetary.

Data: NASA Launch Services Program
