📊 NASA Open Datasets

Free datasets for research, analysis, and space exploration projects

🌍 Earth Observation

NASA Earthdata FREE

Gateway to all Earth science data — atmospheric, oceanic, land, cryo from Terra, Aqua, Landsat, ICESat-2, GRACE-FO.

earthdata.nasa.gov ↗

NASA POWER API FREE

Worldwide weather data — solar, temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation from satellites.

power.larc.nasa.gov ↗

FIRMS — Fire Data FREE

Real-time active fire detection from MODIS/VIIRS. Global within 3 hours.

firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov ↗

NASA EONET API FREE

Earth Observatory Natural Event Tracker — live wildfires, storms, floods, volcanoes and other natural events. No key needed.

eonet.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

Giovanni FREE

Browser-based Earth science analysis — temperature, rainfall, aerosols, ocean chlorophyll.

giovanni.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

🪐 Planetary Science

Planetary Data System FREE

Official archive for all mission data — Mars rovers, Cassini, Juno, New Horizons, MESSENGER.

pds.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open APIs API FREE KEY

APOD, Mars Rover Photos, NEO, DONKI, EPIC, Exoplanet Archive. Free key, 1000 req/hr.

api.nasa.gov ↗

Planetary Fact Sheets FREE

Authoritative data for all planets — mass, gravity, atmosphere, temperature, orbital parameters.

nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

JPL Solar System Dynamics API FREE

Orbits, ephemerides, physical params. Horizons API for trajectories.

ssd.jpl.nasa.gov ↗

🌌 Astronomy

Exoplanet Archive API FREE

5,000+ exoplanets — orbits, mass, radius, host stars. TAP query service.

exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu ↗

MAST Archive FREE

Hubble, JWST, Kepler, TESS — images, spectra, light curves.

mast.stsci.edu ↗

HEASARC FREE

X-ray/gamma-ray from Chandra, Swift, Fermi. Black holes, neutron stars.

heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov ↗

🛰️ Technology & Imagery

NASA Image Library API FREE

140,000+ images, 14,000+ videos. Hubble, JWST, Apollo, Mars, ISS.

images.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open Data Portal FREE

40,000+ datasets — climate, aeronautics, space, software, patents.

data.nasa.gov ↗

NASA Open Source Software FREE

Flight software, simulation, analysis tools. Open for reuse.

code.nasa.gov ↗

TechPort API FREE

2,500+ active technology projects — TRL, descriptions, status.

techport.nasa.gov ↗

🌐 Other Space Datasets & Sources

Beyond NASA — datasets from space agencies and research institutions worldwide

🛰️ International Space Agencies

ESA Gaia Archive FREE

European Space Agency's star catalog — precise positions, distances, and motions of 1.8 billion stars. The most detailed 3D map of our galaxy.

cosmos.esa.int/gaia ↗

ESA Image Archive FREE

European Space Agency's imagery — Mars Express, Rosetta, Gaia, Euclid, and Earth observation missions.

esa.int ↗

JAXA DARTS FREE

Japan's space science archive — Hayabusa asteroid samples, Akatsuki Venus data, X-ray astronomy.

darts.isas.jaxa.jp ↗

🔭 Astronomy Databases

Sloan Digital Sky Survey FREE

The most ambitious sky survey — detailed images and spectra of millions of galaxies, stars, and quasars. Maps the large-scale structure of the universe.

sdss.org ↗

SIMBAD Astronomical Database FREE

Reference database for astronomical objects beyond the solar system — 12+ million objects with cross-identifications and bibliography.

simbad.u-strasbg.fr ↗

Space Telescope Science Institute FREE

Operations center for Hubble and JWST. Vast archive of astronomical observations and data products.

archive.stsci.edu ↗

Zooniverse — Citizen Science FREE

Help classify galaxies, find exoplanets, map the Moon. Real research data you can contribute to analyzing.

zooniverse.org ↗

🌍 Earth & Climate

ESA Climate Change Initiative FREE

Long-term satellite climate records — sea level, ice sheets, greenhouse gases, land cover.

climate.esa.int ↗

Copernicus (EU) API FREE

European Earth observation — Sentinel satellites. Atmosphere, marine, land, climate, emergency, security data.

copernicus.eu ↗

Minor Planet Center FREE
